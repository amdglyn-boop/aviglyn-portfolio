I’m Avi, an aspiring 3D artist. I started learning on 28 June 2026, and this site is basically my public notebook: what I’m making, what I’m struggling with, what I’ve paused and what’s finally starting to click.

—

days into learning 3D

Right now

Three things I’m focused on.

01

Character creation + anatomy

This is the big one. I’m mainly working in ZBrush, with Blender alongside it, trying to understand form and anatomy instead of just chasing detail.

02

Rigging + animation

I want the things I make to actually move. I’m learning the fundamentals in Blender and gradually connecting modelling decisions to how a character will deform and animate.

03

Game development

I’m starting to bring assets into Unreal Engine and learn the pipeline around them. I’ve got game ideas I’d love to build once the 3D fundamentals are strong enough to support them.

Why public?

Accountability, mostly.

I want to keep this honest and real. I’m not building a site that pretends I’m already at the finish line. I want somewhere that makes it harder to quietly stop.

The goal is to broadcast the process as much as I can: post work, take feedback, learn from the community, get involved in ZBrushCentral, enter competitions, put things in front of people and keep trying to make something of it.
