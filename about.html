02 / Why this exists

I want the progress to be visible while it’s happening, not rewritten later when everything looks polished.

I’m not trying to make this site look like I’ve been doing 3D for ten years. I haven’t.

I do want the site itself to feel considered, though. The work can be rough while I’m learning; the way I document it doesn’t have to be.

The idea is simple: keep making things, put them somewhere public, enter competitions when I can, talk to more artists, and make it harder to quietly give up when something gets difficult.
