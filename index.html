Theme + references

Break down the brief, pick an idea and gather references before touching the sculpt.

02

Week two

Blockout

Get the big forms working. No hiding weak shapes under detail.

03

Week three

Detail + presentation

Refine the piece, solve the materials and start thinking about the final images.

04

Week four

Finish + submit

Call it done, render it properly and actually put it out into the world.
